Sample Stories by Age

Every SLEEPO story is personalized to your child — but these samples give you a feel for the tone, themes, and focus areas we craft stories around at each age.

🎵
A note on story length: each story ends with about 4 minutes of soft, soothing instrumental music that gradually fades out — no narration, just calming music to help your child drift off to sleep. The story itself is shorter than the full running time you'll see on the player.
