Sleepo Kids — Alexa Skill Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This page describes the additional information SLEEPO collects when a parent links their SLEEPO account to the "Sleepo Kids" Alexa skill. It supplements our main Privacy Policy, which covers the SLEEPO app and website generally.

What We Collect Through the Alexa Skill

  • A linking code - spoken by the skill and entered by you at app.sleepokids.org/parent/alexa-link while logged into your own SLEEPO account — this connects your Amazon device to your SLEEPO account without the skill ever asking for or collecting any personal information via voice.
  • Story playback information — which story was played and your playback position (so you can resume where you left off) — this is new data generated each time you use the skill.

We do not collect your child's voice recordings, and Amazon does not share any Alexa voice-request data with us beyond what's needed to fulfill the request. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Data Retention

Playback events recorded via the Sleepo Kids Alexa skill (story ID, title, and playback position) are stored in the same database and under the same retention policy as story-activity data generated through the SLEEPO web app — retained indefinitely as part of your child's account history, not subject to a separate or shorter retention period for Alexa specifically.
