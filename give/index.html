Your time can help a child grow with confidence, courage, and kindness.

Bedtime is where children often feel safest, and where the stories they hear help shape who they become. SLEEPO Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that creates personalized bedtime stories for children ages 2–12.

Stories That Shape

This Microsoft GIVE season, give a few minutes to review a story, record one, answer a short survey, or share your skills, and be part of a child’s bedtime story.

Why your contribution matters

A human voice and a careful review can make a bedtime story feel personal.

SLEEPO’s team listens to recordings and reviews submitted work before it reaches a family. Volunteers add time, judgment, and warmth to that process.
