Microsoft GIVE 2026 × SLEEPO Foundation

Give your time. Help shape a child’s bedtime story.

SLEEPO Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit helping children ages 2–12 build confidence, positive values, and cultural connection through personalized bedtime stories.

During Microsoft GIVE 2026, employees can help by recording, reviewing, and shaping stories that may become part of a child’s bedtime routine.

A simple volunteer flow

Choose an activity and contribute in the time you have.

Most activities can be completed in a single sitting and repeated with different stories.

1

Review the participation terms

Read SLEEPO’s volunteer expectations covering confidentiality, appropriate use, and permission to use your contribution.

2

Pick an activity

Choose from recording a story, reviewing a story or audio story, and additional activities as they become available.

3

Complete it and log your time

Follow the on-screen instructions, submit your contribution, and log your eligible volunteer time in Benevity.

One example: record a bedtime story. Most stories take about five to ten minutes to read, plus a few minutes to settle in and listen back. SLEEPO reviews recordings before they are shared with families.
Why your contribution matters

A human voice and a careful review can make a bedtime story feel personal.

SLEEPO’s team listens to recordings and reviews submitted work before it reaches a family. Volunteers add time, judgment, and warmth to that process.
