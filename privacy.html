Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

At SLEEPO Foundation, your family's privacy is fundamental to everything we do.
This page explains what we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

Who We Are

SLEEPO Foundation is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Redmond, Washington, USA. We create parent-guided, personalized bedtime audio stories for children ages 2–12, designed to support confidence, life skills, emotional strength, family connection, and cultural traditions.

This Privacy Policy covers SLEEPO websites and parent-facing services, including sleepofoundation.org, sleepo.org, and services on sleepokids.org. Parents or legal guardians provide information about their children and control the story experience.

What Information We Collect

Information About Your Child

  • Child's name, age, and, when provided, gender
  • Interests, favorites, personal traits, cultural traditions, and family details you choose to share
  • Strengths, growth areas, life skills, and goals you want stories to explore
  • Personalized story text, approved stories, narration recordings, and feedback associated with your child's profile

Information About You

  • Parent or guardian name and relationship to the child
  • Email address, WhatsApp number or other contact details you provide
  • Location details such as city, state, and country
  • Enrollment, payment status, and communication information needed to operate the program

Story Listening and Access Information

To provide story access and apply the complimentary listening policy after paid enrollment ends, SLEEPO uses information about when stories are played and the date of the most recent qualifying listen. We also maintain information about enrollment and story-access status.

Narration and Voice Recordings

Families may choose SLEEPO Narrator (AI voice), an AI-generated narration option, or have a parent or other family member record a story. A family recording includes the speaker's voice and the resulting story audio. Recordings are used only to create, store, and play the story for the family.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create, personalize, and deliver stories based on information a parent or guardian supplies
  • To let parents review and approve stories before narration, and to provide SLEEPO Narrator or family recordings
  • To manage each child's free trial, monthly participation fee, story delivery, and Family Voice Bonus
  • To maintain story libraries, process listening activity, and apply the complimentary access and retention rules described below
  • To communicate with families about stories, approvals, payments, service updates, and support requests
  • To maintain security, operate the nonprofit program, and improve reliability and content quality

Family Voice Bonus eligibility is based on whether a family member records at least one of the child's stories during the applicable week. SLEEPO adds one bonus personalized story for that week when the condition is met.

Legal Basis for Processing

We use personal information to provide the family-requested program, communicate with parents and guardians, manage family choices and permissions, safeguard the Service, and meet applicable legal requirements. Where consent is required by law, we obtain the applicable consent and honor parental requests to review, stop further collection, or delete children's personal information as described below.

What We Never Do

  • Sell children's or families' personal information
  • Share children's or families' personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes
  • Invite children to register for their own accounts or submit personal information directly to SLEEPO
  • Publish a child's personalized story, recording, or profile as a public sample or promotion

Children's profiles are created and managed by parents or legal guardians. SLEEPO uses service providers as explained below to operate the program.

Third-Party Services

SLEEPO uses service providers as needed to operate its websites and parent program, including providers for:

  • Website hosting, cloud storage, databases, and account access
  • AI-assisted story creation and AI-generated narration
  • Storage and playback of family-recorded and narrator-generated audio
  • Payment processing and applicable tax calculation
  • Service communications and website performance monitoring

Service providers may process information on SLEEPO's behalf according to their applicable service arrangements and privacy practices; processing may occur outside your state or country. We do not share family information with these providers for their independent advertising or marketing.

We use privacy-focused website analytics to understand general usage patterns. If you have questions about a particular service provider or the information it handles, contact us using the details below.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect family information. Data transmitted through our websites is protected using HTTPS encryption, and access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers who need it to operate the program. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Data Retention

While Your Child Is Enrolled

We keep profile information, stories, and narration recordings as needed to provide the program and enable listening, subject to parental deletion rights and applicable legal requirements.

After You Cancel

Cancellation ends future paid enrollment after the current paid month. Your child continues to receive new stories through the end of that paid month. Afterward, the family may continue listening to existing stories at no additional cost, provided it listens to at least one existing story every 60 days. Each qualifying listen starts a new 60-day period. New personalized stories require paid enrollment.

If There Is No Listening for 60 Consecutive Days

Complimentary listening access ends. Your child's existing stories and recordings are retained for an additional 90 days, during which you may re-enroll at the participation fee available at that time and restore access.

After the Additional 90 Days

If you do not re-enroll during that additional retention period, the stories and recordings will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Your child's profile information and listening history will be deleted on the same schedule as stories and recordings, except where a legal requirement applies. Account and billing records are retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes, then deleted in accordance with applicable law.

If You Do Not Continue After the Free Trial

If you do not choose paid enrollment after the free trial, your child's profile information, stories, recordings, and listening history are permanently deleted 30 days after the trial ends, except for information that must be retained by law.

Requests to Delete Information Earlier

A parent or guardian can request review or deletion of their child's personal information before those deadlines. Deletion requests are typically completed within 30 days, unless a legal retention requirement or another applicable exception prevents immediate deletion. Deleting information needed to personalize stories may prevent further program participation. Contact us using the information below.

For participation fees and cancellation conditions, see our Terms of Use and Participation Fee page.

Parental Rights Under COPPA

SLEEPO is parent-guided and serves families with children under 13. Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), parents and legal guardians may exercise applicable rights concerning information collected about their children.

You may contact us to:

  • Review information SLEEPO maintains about your child
  • Request correction or deletion of your child's information and recordings
  • Refuse further collection or use of your child's information

We may need to verify that the request comes from an authorized parent or guardian before disclosing or deleting information. If you request deletion of information necessary to create or deliver stories, we may no longer be able to provide the personalized story experience.

Your Rights

Parents and guardians may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held about their family. To make a request, email amit@sleepofoundation.org. We may verify your identity or parental authority before acting on a request. Rights and exceptions may depend on applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our program evolves or as required by law. We will update the date at the top of the page and, when appropriate or legally required, notify families of material changes and obtain any necessary consent.

Questions or Requests?

Reach out any time — we're happy to answer questions about your family's data or help you exercise your privacy rights.
