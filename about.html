About Us

SLEEPO Foundation is a parent-founded nonprofit built on a simple belief — that the children of today will shape the world of tomorrow, and that stories can play a meaningful role in shaping their lives.

Stories That Shape

The Founders' Commitment

After 30 years of hard work in technology and corporate life, and raising two wonderful children, Amit and Dipti Goyal decided to dedicate the next chapter of their lives to giving back to society. After years of careful thought and planning, they stepped away from their corporate careers to build SLEEPO and contribute to shaping the next generation.

How SLEEPO Started

For nearly three decades, our founder, Amit, worked in technology and sales — but writing had always been a quiet passion. About eight years ago, he began thinking seriously about creating something that could make a meaningful difference in the lives of children and families, beyond any one community or country.

Years of travel across the United States, Canada, Europe, and Asia, together with ongoing research into sleep, childhood development, and personal growth, gradually shaped that idea.

At the heart of this journey were three fundamental beliefs.

Children of today will shape the world of tomorrow. The formative years between ages 2 and 12 offer an extraordinary opportunity to nurture self-confidence, emotional strength, positive habits, life skills, and strong character — helping children grow into good human beings and responsible citizens.

Stories have the power to shape lives. Children connect naturally with stories. Through characters, adventures, challenges, and experiences they can relate to, stories can inspire curiosity, encourage positive thinking, help children overcome challenges, and introduce important life lessons in ways that feel natural and enjoyable. This belief is reflected in our tagline — Stories That Shape.

Sleep and bedtime hold a special place in childhood. Sleep is essential to a child's physical, emotional, and cognitive development. The quiet moments before sleep also offer a unique opportunity for children to listen, reflect, imagine, and connect with their families. This inspired both the name SLEEPO and our decision to begin our journey with bedtime stories.

Together, these beliefs became the foundation for SLEEPO.

In 2023, Amit stepped away from his corporate career of nearly 30 years to focus on building SLEEPO full-time, beginning with personalized bedtime audio stories for children ages 2–12.

His wife, Dipti, then a Principal Engineering Manager at Microsoft, supported the early work alongside her full-time career — helping review stories, record narration, test ideas, and shape the technology behind the program.

In 2025, after more than 20 years at Microsoft, Dipti also left her corporate career to join SLEEPO full-time as co-founder. Today, she leads SLEEPO's technology and plays an active role in community outreach, program development, and growth.

What began as an idea explored over many years has become a shared family mission: to help children develop the confidence, character, values, and life skills they need to thrive in life and contribute positively to the world around them.

Personalized bedtime audio stories are the first step in this journey. As SLEEPO grows, reaches more families, and learns from their experiences, we look forward to exploring new and meaningful ways to support children's development.

SLEEPO has become, in every sense, Amit and Dipti's third child — something they have poured their time, energy, and hearts into building. For them, the journey doesn't feel like a sacrifice; it feels like they've finally found their calling.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help children ages 2–12 build self-confidence, develop essential life skills, cultivate positive habits, strengthen their emotional resilience, build strong character, and stay connected to their cultural roots and values — empowering them to grow into confident, compassionate, capable, and responsible individuals.

We believe every child is unique, and that their developmental journey should reflect their individual personality, interests, strengths, challenges, and family values.

Our first initiative brings this mission to life through personalized bedtime audio stories, thoughtfully created for each child in partnership with their parents.

SLEEPO Foundation is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Redmond, Washington, USA.

Our Vision

A world where every child has the opportunity to develop the confidence, character, values, and life skills to realize their full potential and contribute to a better world — regardless of their background, geography, or family's financial circumstances.

As a nonprofit, we are committed to making our programs accessible to as many children as possible. Support from donors, volunteers, and community partners helps us keep participation affordable and provide financial assistance to families who need it, subject to available resources.
