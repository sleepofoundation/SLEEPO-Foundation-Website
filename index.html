Our Mission

SLEEPO Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit helping children ages 2–12 build confidence, positive values, and cultural connection through personalized bedtime stories—strengthening children and families, one story at a time.

Childhood Is Changing Fast

Children today are growing up in a world of constant stimulation, shorter attention spans, busy schedules, digital distractions, and fewer quiet family moments.

Yet kindness, curiosity, empathy, and confidence are becoming more important than ever.

SLEEPO helps families intentionally nurture these qualities through calm, deeply personalized bedtime storytelling.

Why Bedtime Matters

Bedtime offers something rare in a child’s busy day—a quiet moment to slow down, feel connected, and simply listen.

In this calm setting, meaningful stories and positive affirmations can gently explore confidence, kindness, resilience, values, and the experiences shaping your child’s world.

That’s why SLEEPO is built around bedtime—turning a quiet moment at the end of the day into something meaningful your child can look forward to.
