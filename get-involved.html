Get Involved

SLEEPO is a growing, all-volunteer nonprofit—and we welcome people who believe in our mission and want to help us reach more children and families.

Interested in Helping SLEEPO Grow?

As a growing nonprofit, we welcome experienced volunteers and advisors who would like to contribute their time, skills, and perspective.

We're especially interested in people with experience in:

  • Marketing & Communications
  • Social Media & Digital Outreach
  • Nonprofit Strategy
  • Legal Guidance
  • Fundraising & Partnerships
  • Technology
  • Community Outreach

You don't need to make a large time commitment. Even a few hours of your time or occasional guidance can make a meaningful difference as SLEEPO grows.

Ways to Get Involved

Volunteer Your Skills

Help with a specific project or area where your experience can make a difference.

Become an Advisor

Share your professional perspective and help SLEEPO think thoughtfully about its growth and future.

Help Us Reach More Families

Introduce SLEEPO to families, schools, community organizations, or others who may connect with our mission.

We'd Love to Hear From You

If SLEEPO's mission speaks to you and you think your experience could help, we'd love to start a conversation. There's no formal commitment required to reach out.

Reach out anytime—you'll hear directly from the SLEEPO team.
