Getting Started

What is SLEEPO?

SLEEPO is a nonprofit personalized storytelling program for children and families. We create bedtime stories inspired by each child's age, interests, strengths, culture, experiences, and growth goals. The stories are designed to support confidence, character, healthy habits, emotional awareness, cultural connection, and meaningful family time.

What makes SLEEPO different from ordinary children's stories?

Instead of giving every child the same story, SLEEPO builds stories around an individual child. A story may reflect the child's name, favorite activities, family traditions, talents, challenges, or a life lesson selected by the parent. The goal is for children to recognize their own world in the story and connect more deeply with its message.

How does the program work?

  1. A parent or legal guardian enrolls the child and completes the SLEEPO Success Matrix.
  2. SLEEPO creates personalized bedtime stories based on the child's profile and your goals.
  3. You review and approve each story.
  4. You choose SLEEPO Narrator or record the story yourself (or with another family member).
  5. Your child listens to the finished audio story; you can share feedback and update preferences over time.

While enrolled, your child receives 3 new personalized stories every week, with the opportunity to receive a Family Voice Bonus.

How do I sign up?

Visit register.sleepo.org to create your child's profile and complete the SLEEPO Success Matrix. You'll start with a 2-week free trial, with no credit card required.

What does the free trial include?

Your child's first 6 personalized bedtime stories over 2 weeks are free. You can review the stories and choose SLEEPO Narrator or family narration. No credit card is required. After the trial, you decide whether to continue for $15 per month per child.

How long until our first story arrives?

Most families receive their first personalized story within a day or two after completing the Success Matrix.

What ages is SLEEPO built for?

SLEEPO stories are designed for children roughly ages 2–12, with story tone and complexity adjusted to your child's age.

Is SLEEPO only for bedtime?

No. Bedtime is a natural time for calm listening and family connection, but stories can also be enjoyed during quiet time, car rides, travel, or other family moments.

Are the stories audio or written?

SLEEPO is designed around personalized bedtime audio stories. You receive the written story to review and approve before it's narrated, then your child can listen to the finished recording.

How often will my child receive stories?

While enrolled, your child receives 3 new personalized stories every week. If you or another family member records at least one story during the week, we'll add one bonus personalized story for your child that week, at no additional cost. If you would like additional stories, contact SLEEPO to ask about availability.

Can we listen to a story more than once?

Yes. Children often enjoy returning to familiar stories, and repeated listening can reinforce positive messages and make the story part of a comforting routine.

How much does SLEEPO cost?

Start with 2 weeks free (your child's first 6 personalized stories), with no credit card required. After the trial, you can choose to continue for $15 per month per child. You are not automatically charged for beginning the trial. Any applicable taxes will be shown before you authorize payment.

Why is it called a participation fee?

SLEEPO is a nonprofit family program, not a commercial storytelling service. The $15 monthly participation fee helps cover the costs of creating, narrating, storing, and delivering your child's personalized stories. Donations and volunteer support cover the remaining costs, helping keep the fee affordable for families.

Why was the monthly fee reduced from $25 to $15?

Contributions from individual donors and volunteers, including support received through Microsoft Give, have helped us reduce SLEEPO's monthly participation fee from $25 to $15 per child. This support helps more families access personalized bedtime stories.

What is included in the monthly participation fee?

  • 3 new personalized stories every week
  • Your choice of SLEEPO Narrator or narration recorded by a family member
  • Parent review and approval of stories before narration
  • A Family Voice Bonus when a family member records at least one story during the week
  • Replay your child's stories anytime while enrolled

What is the Family Voice Bonus?

When you or another family member records at least one of your child's stories during the week, we'll add one bonus personalized story for your child that week, at no additional cost. Recording is completely optional. You can always choose SLEEPO Narrator.

How much does it cost to enroll multiple children?

The same fee applies to each child: $15/month per enrolled child. For example, two children cost $30/month; three cost $45/month. Each child receives stories personalized to their own profile.

Will I be charged automatically after the free trial?

No. You do not provide a credit card to start the trial. After it ends, you can decide whether to continue with the $15 monthly participation fee per child. Billing begins only after you authorize payment. Any applicable taxes will be shown before you authorize payment.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel anytime through your SLEEPO parent portal. There is no long-term commitment. Your enrollment remains active through the end of your current paid month, so your child will continue receiving new stories until then. You won't be charged again. Afterward, you can continue listening to existing stories for free under our complimentary story-access policy.

Can we still listen to stories after cancellation?

Yes. After your paid month ends, you can continue listening to your child's existing stories at no additional cost as long as your family listens to at least one existing story every 60 days. Each listen starts a new 60-day period. New personalized stories require paid enrollment.

What happens if we don't listen for 60 days?

If your family does not listen to a story for 60 consecutive days, complimentary listening access ends. Your child's stories and recordings are retained for an additional 90 days so you can re-enroll and restore access. If you do not re-enroll during that additional 90-day period, the stories and recordings will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Can we return to SLEEPO or restore our stories?

Yes. You can re-enroll at the participation fee available at that time (currently $15/month per child). If your child's stories and recordings are still within the additional 90-day retention period, they can be restored. Permanently deleted stories and recordings cannot be recovered.

Where do participation fees go?

The fee helps pay for the personalized story creation and delivery provided to your child, including customization, narration, and the technology required to store and play the stories. Donations and volunteer time help support SLEEPO's shared platform, development, outreach, and other nonprofit expenses. Learn more on our Participation Fee page.

Is financial assistance available?

Sponsored participation or financial assistance may be available depending on donations and program resources. Families who need support can contact SLEEPO privately at amit@sleepofoundation.org.

Are participation fees tax-deductible?

The monthly participation fee is payment for your child's personalized stories, not a tax-deductible charitable donation. Separate donations to SLEEPO Foundation may be tax-deductible as permitted by law.

Personalization

What information do you use in stories?

Your child's name, interests, and the strengths, growth areas, life skills and values, and passions you share through the Success Matrix — details that make each story feel written specifically for them.

Do I have to answer every profile question?

No. Share only what you are comfortable providing. More detail can make a story feel more personal, but please do not submit highly sensitive information such as passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, medical records, or exact daily schedules.

Can I update my child's profile?

Yes. Children's interests and needs change. Parents can provide new preferences, goals, or feedback so future stories continue to feel relevant.

Can parents choose a lesson or theme?

Yes. Parents can suggest a skill, habit, family value, or situation for a story to explore — such as confidence, friendship, kindness, perseverance, organization, healthy habits, cultural traditions, school experiences, or trying something new. We aim to communicate lessons through enjoyable stories.

Can stories reflect our family's culture and traditions?

Yes — we design with diverse families in mind and personalize stories to reflect your family's traditions and values wherever possible.

Are stories available in different languages?

Our current personalized storytelling program is available in English. We hope to offer additional languages as the program grows. If another language is important to your family, please let us know.

Can siblings or other family members appear in a story?

Yes, when requested by a parent or guardian. Siblings, parents, grandparents, and other important people can be included using appropriate details supplied by the family. Families may also request a shared sibling story.

Can a story use my child's favorite copyrighted character?

You may tell us what kinds of characters your child enjoys. To respect intellectual-property rights, SLEEPO may create an original character with similar positive qualities instead of reproducing a protected character, brand, or story world.

Can I request a story about a difficult situation?

Parents may request age-appropriate stories about experiences such as starting school, making friends, moving, sibling disagreements, performance nerves, or developing a new habit. SLEEPO can support family conversations, but it is not a substitute for medical, psychological, educational, or legal care.

What if my child does not connect with a story?

Tell us what did and did not work — the topic, pace, style, characters, language, or personalization. Parent feedback helps SLEEPO better understand your child's preferences and improve future stories.

In whose voice can the story be recorded?

You can choose whichever narration option works best for your family:

  • SLEEPO Narrator (AI voice): Our narrator can read all your child's stories, so you don't need to record anything yourself.
  • Family Voice: You or another family member can record stories in your own voices, adding a familiar, personal touch to bedtime.

Both options are included in your monthly participation fee.

Children & Siblings

Can I enroll more than one child?

Yes. Each enrolled child will have an individual Success Matrix and stories personalized to their age, interests, and goals.

Will siblings receive the same story?

No. Each child will receive stories tailored to their profile. Parents may request a shared sibling story when appropriate.

What if my children are different ages?

That is completely fine. Vocabulary, story structure, themes, and length are adjusted for each child's age and developmental level.

Privacy & Trust

Is SLEEPO safe for children?

SLEEPO is designed as a parent-guided program. A parent or legal guardian enrolls the child, decides what information to share, and can provide feedback. We aim for age-appropriate, respectful, encouraging stories.

Does my child communicate directly with SLEEPO?

Never. Only parents or guardians interact with the SLEEPO team.

Is my child's information private?

Information is used to personalize and deliver your child's stories. SLEEPO does not sell children's or families' personal information. For details about collection, storage, sharing, correction, and deletion, please read our Privacy Page.

Will my child's story be shared publicly?

A personalized story is accessible only to the parent or guardian of your child. Your child's story will not be used publicly as a sample, testimonial, promotion, or social-media post.

Does SLEEPO use artificial intelligence?

Yes. SLEEPO uses technology, including AI-assisted tools, to help develop personalized stories and deliver the listening experience. SLEEPO Narrator provides AI-generated narration; family members can instead choose to record stories themselves. Parents review and approve the story before narration, and SLEEPO maintains content and privacy safeguards.

Are stories reviewed before children receive them?

Yes. SLEEPO applies quality and safety standards to personalized stories, and parents review and approve each story before narration. We encourage shared listening, particularly when a story addresses a sensitive or family-specific situation.

Is SLEEPO a nonprofit?

Yes — SLEEPO Foundation is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 39-4188744.

Is SLEEPO therapy or medical treatment?

SLEEPO is an educational and family storytelling program, not a medical or therapeutic service. SLEEPO does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical, developmental, behavioral, mental-health, or sleep conditions. Parents with concerns about their child's health, development, behavior, mental health, or sleep should consult an appropriately qualified professional.

Can SLEEPO guarantee a particular result or make my child fall asleep?

No. Stories can support a calm routine, reflection, connection, imagination, and positive family conversations, but SLEEPO cannot guarantee sleep or a specific behavioral, educational, emotional, or health outcome.

Listening & Parent Support

Does my child need to use a screen?

No, SLEEPO is completely audio based. Many parents use Bluetooth speakers or a parent can start the story and place the device aside or turn off the display.

Should a parent listen with the child?

Shared listening is encouraged whenever possible. It can create opportunities for connection, reassurance, and simple conversations about the story. Older children may also listen independently based on the parent's judgment.

What can we talk about regarding the story, the next morning or later?

Try one relaxed question: “What was your favorite part?”, “How did the character feel?”, “What would you have done?”, or “What should happen next?” A warm, brief conversation is enough.

What happens after enrollment?

You'll complete your child's SLEEPO Success Matrix, begin your first two weeks and six personalized stories free, and receive story review and narration instructions. If you choose to continue after the trial, the current participation fee is $15/month per child. You can update story preferences and share feedback as your child grows.

Can schools or community organizations work with SLEEPO?

Yes. SLEEPO welcomes conversations with schools, libraries, cultural organizations, community groups, and family associations. Feel free to reach out to us to discuss a partnership.

Can I donate without enrolling a child?

Yes. Donations help expand access, strengthen the program, and bring personalized stories to more children.

How can I contact SLEEPO?

Use our Contact page. Please do not include highly sensitive personal, medical, financial, or identifying information in a general message.
