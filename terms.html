The Program

SLEEPO creates age-appropriate bedtime audio stories personalized using information provided by parents and guardians. Stories are intended for private, personal, non-commercial family listening.

While enrolled in paid participation, each child receives 3 new personalized stories every week. SLEEPO uses AI-assisted tools to help write personalized stories, and parents review and approve story text before narration. Families may select SLEEPO Narrator (AI voice), our AI-generated narration option, or record stories in their own voices. Both are included in the monthly participation fee.

Family Voice Bonus: If a parent or other family member records at least one of the child's stories during a week, SLEEPO adds one bonus personalized story for that child during that week at no additional cost. Recording is optional; families may choose SLEEPO Narrator for every story.

Story length, style, and delivery timing may vary with the child's age, parent approval, and technical circumstances. We will make reasonable efforts to deliver the stated weekly story allocation and communicate material interruptions.

Two-Week Free Trial and Participation Fee

A new child may begin with 2 weeks free, including the child's first 6 personalized stories. A credit card is not required to start the trial. Starting the trial does not automatically begin paid enrollment.

After the trial, a parent may choose to continue at the currently published $15 monthly participation fee per child. Each enrolled child has a separate personalized story experience. For example, enrollment for two children costs $30 per month before any applicable taxes.

Payment begins only after the parent authorizes payment. After a family chooses to continue, the monthly participation fee recurs until the family cancels. Applicable taxes, if charged, are displayed before payment authorization. Fees paid for personalized stories are not charitable donations; separate donations to SLEEPO Foundation may be tax-deductible as permitted by law.

SLEEPO may change the participation fee in the future. We will notify enrolled families at least 30 days before a fee change takes effect, and you may cancel before the new fee applies. Any future fee shown in the parent portal or at checkout controls for new or renewed paid participation.

Cancellation and Complimentary Story Access

Cancellation of Paid Enrollment

Parents may cancel anytime through the SLEEPO parent portal, without a long-term commitment. Paid enrollment remains active through the end of the current paid month, and the child continues receiving new stories until then. After cancellation takes effect, you won't be charged again unless you choose to re-enroll. Fees already paid are not refunded for partial months.

Complimentary Listening After the Paid Month

After paid enrollment ends, existing stories remain available to listen to at no additional cost as long as the family listens to at least one existing story every 60 days. Each listen starts a new 60-day period. New personalized stories require paid enrollment.

Access Ends After 60 Days Without Listening

If the family does not listen to a story for 60 consecutive days, complimentary access ends. The child's existing stories and recordings are kept for an additional 90 days so the parent may re-enroll and restore access.

Permanent Deletion After the Additional 90 Days

If the parent does not re-enroll during the additional 90-day period, the child's stories and recordings will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Re-enrollment uses the participation fee available at that time. Parents may separately request earlier deletion in accordance with the Privacy Policy.
