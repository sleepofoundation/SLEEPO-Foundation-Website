What's Included

What does my child receive?

Each enrolled child receives 3 new personalized bedtime stories every week, created around their age, interests, experiences, and the areas you want to nurture. You review and approve each story before narration.

For audio, you can choose SLEEPO Narrator, our AI-generated narration option, or have a parent or another family member record the story. You can listen to existing stories again whenever you wish while you have access.

Family Voice Bonus

When you or another family member records at least one of your child's stories during the week, we'll add one bonus personalized story for your child that week, at no additional cost. Recording is optional; you can choose SLEEPO Narrator for all your child's stories.

What happens during the free trial?

Start with 2 weeks free and your child's first 6 personalized stories. No credit card is required. When the trial ends, you can choose whether to continue at $15/month per child; starting the free trial does not automatically begin paid enrollment.

Why a Participation Fee?

Why is it called a participation fee?

SLEEPO is a nonprofit family program, not a commercial storytelling service. The $15 monthly participation fee helps cover the costs of creating, narrating, storing, and delivering your child's personalized stories. Donations and volunteer support cover the remaining costs, helping keep the fee affordable for families.

Why was the fee reduced from $25 to $15?

Contributions from individual donors and volunteers, including support received through Microsoft Give, have helped us reduce SLEEPO's monthly participation fee from $25 to $15 per child. This support helps more families access personalized bedtime stories.

How does pricing work for more than one child?

The fee is $15 per month for each enrolled child. Each child has an individual profile and personalized stories.

Are participation fees tax-deductible?

The monthly participation fee is payment for your child's personalized stories, not a tax-deductible charitable donation. Separate donations to SLEEPO Foundation may be tax-deductible as permitted by law.

Cancellation & Story Access

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel anytime through your SLEEPO parent portal. There is no long-term commitment. Your enrollment remains active through the end of your current paid month, so your child will continue receiving new stories until then. You won't be charged again. Afterward, you can continue listening to existing stories for free under our complimentary story-access policy.

Can we still listen to our stories after cancelling?

Yes. After your paid month ends, you can continue listening to your child's existing stories at no additional cost as long as your family listens to at least one existing story every 60 days. Each listen starts a new 60-day period. New personalized stories require paid enrollment.

What if we do not listen for 60 days?

If your family does not listen to a story for 60 consecutive days, complimentary listening access ends. Your child's stories and recordings are retained for an additional 90 days so you can re-enroll and restore access. If you do not re-enroll during that additional 90-day period, the stories and recordings will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Can we return to SLEEPO later?

Yes. You can re-enroll at the participation fee available at that time (currently $15/month per child). If your child's stories and recordings are still within the additional 90-day retention period, they can be restored. Permanently deleted stories and recordings cannot be recovered.
